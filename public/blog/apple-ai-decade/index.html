Apple's been playing a different game since 2017

While everyone rented Nvidia GPUs and called it infrastructure, Apple quietly built AI silicon before AI was the thing to build for.

01

Apple put the entire iPhone Air motherboard inside the camera bump: CPU, everything. The rest is screen and battery. Nobody noticed. Everyone was busy complaining about the charging cable.

02

The AI race didn't start when everyone thinks it did. While the whole industry was renting Nvidia GPUs and calling it infrastructure, Apple was quietly building purpose-built AI silicon into every device they shipped. Since 2017. ChatGPT didn't exist. The AI boom was years away.

"Everyone else built hardware, then figured out how to make AI run on it. Apple built hardware specific to AI before AI was the thing to build for."

03

The companies that win the next decade of AI aren't going to have the best model. They're going to own the full stack: chip, OS, software, all optimized together. Apple's been doing that quietly for almost a decade. Full breakdown on Medium.
